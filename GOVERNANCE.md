# Governance

Public Purpose Lab is currently founder led. This lightweight model is intended
for the founding phase and must evolve before the project accepts significant
external contributions or operates a real service.

## Founding participants

- Adrian Sutherland
- Stephen Boyle

The founders jointly steward purpose, scope, public claims, architecture,
licensing, and participation.

## Decision model

- Prefer consensus for material decisions.
- Record lasting architecture and policy decisions as short decision records.
- Use repository issues for proposals, evidence, and unresolved questions.
- Prefer reversible experiments when evidence is incomplete.
- Keep demonstrator-specific product decisions separate from reusable platform
  decisions.

The following require both founders' agreement during the founding phase:

- project and product naming;
- licence and intellectual-property policy;
- use of real organisational or personal data;
- public claims of readiness, safety, compliance, endorsement, or partnership;
- external funding, formal partnerships, or commitments;
- changes to the Lab's purpose or initially excluded clinical scope; and
- granting repository administration or release authority.

## Safety and publication stop

Either founder may pause a demonstration, publication, release, or data use when
there is a credible privacy, confidentiality, security, legal, safety, or
reputational concern. The pause remains until both founders agree that the
concern is resolved or accept a documented residual risk.

## Roles as participation grows

Future governance should distinguish maintainers, domain reviewers, security
reviewers, and contributors. No external participant's involvement should be
presented as institutional endorsement without written authority.

## Licensing gate

No substantive external code contributions will be accepted until code,
documentation, and contribution licensing are agreed and published. Discussion,
issues, and review feedback are welcome in the meantime.
