# Brand foundation

## Brand idea

The identity is built around a **shared purpose point**. Four independent arcs
surround a warm centre. They represent organisations, systems, disciplines, and
people collaborating around a public outcome while retaining clear boundaries
and responsibilities. The gaps signal interoperability, inquiry, and room for
others to participate.

## Name and language

**Name:** Public Purpose Lab
**Descriptor:** An open-source lab for trustworthy service integration and automation.
**Primary strapline:** Open systems. Public purpose.
**Supporting line:** Discover. Connect. Assure.

Use **Public Purpose Lab** on first reference and **the Lab** sparingly after
that. Avoid the abbreviation PPL. Demonstrators and products may receive their
own names when their audience and purpose are clear.

## Voice

- Plain-spoken and concrete.
- Evidence-led about what is proposed, demonstrated, measured, and unknown.
- Humane: people and outcomes before platforms.
- Constructively sceptical, including of the Lab's own claims.
- Open-handed and calmly ambitious.

Avoid “revolutionary,” “guaranteed safe,” “AI transformation,” and “NHS-ready.”
Prefer accurate terms such as “experimental,” “synthetic demonstrator,”
“assurance evidence,” and “designed for evaluation.”

## Colours

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---:|
| Primary | Civic navy | `#17324D` |
| Secondary | Purpose teal | `#1E7A72` |
| Human accent | Warm coral | `#E4775B` |
| Inquiry accent | Signal gold | `#E5AD45` |
| Paper | Warm white | `#F7F3EA` |
| Mist | Civic mist | `#E8F0EF` |
| Body ink | Charcoal | `#26333D` |

Use Inter where available, with Arial, Helvetica, and sans-serif fallbacks. Use
JetBrains Mono or another readable monospace face for code and event examples.

## Logo use

- Use the full wordmark when the audience may not know the Lab.
- Use the mark alone for avatars and small icons.
- Preserve clear space equal to the centre point's diameter.
- Do not close the gaps, rearrange the arcs, or add governmental, NHS, medical,
  flag, or crown styling.
