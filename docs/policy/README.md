# Policy and governance documents

This index identifies the standing public rules under which Public Purpose Lab
operates. These documents govern the programme and its demonstrations; they are
not claims of legal, regulatory, clinical or production certification.

## Policy-document convention

Further public policies should be created when the programme needs a stable,
approved rule that is not already covered. Likely subjects include privacy and
data handling, responsible AI use and assurance, publication and evidence,
accessibility, licensing and intellectual property.

Every policy should identify:

- its status and last review date;
- its scope and explicit exclusions;
- the accountable decision owner;
- required behaviour, evidence and exception handling; and
- the event or date that triggers review.

Website privacy, security and accessibility pages are visitor-facing
explanations. They should reflect these canonical policies but do not replace
them unless the founders explicitly adopt them as project policy.
