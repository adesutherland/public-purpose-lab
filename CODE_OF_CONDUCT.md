# Code of conduct

Public Purpose Lab depends on thoughtful disagreement across technology, public
services, charities, policy, and lived experience. Participation must remain
respectful, evidence led, and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat people with dignity and assume good faith while testing claims.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and effects rather than personal motives.
- Make uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and relevant affiliations visible.
- Respect confidentiality, consent, privacy, and professional boundaries.
- Make space for domain expertise and people affected by the services discussed.
- Correct mistakes openly and without retaliation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualised attention, deliberate
misrepresentation, unauthorised disclosure, or pressure to weaken safety and
privacy controls is not acceptable.

## Response

Raise concerns privately with either founder. The founders may remove content,
pause participation, restrict access, or take other proportionate action. A
founder involved in a concern should not decide its outcome alone.
