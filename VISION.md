# Vision and mission

## Vision

Public-purpose organisations can improve services without surrendering control
of their data, decisions, or future to unaffordable or opaque technology.

## Mission

Build open, trustworthy, and demonstrable building blocks that help charities
and public services:

1. discover how fragmented work and information actually flow;
2. connect systems through explicit, replaceable interfaces;
3. automate bounded operational workflows with accountable human control; and
4. retain evidence showing what happened, why it happened, and how confidently
   the result should be treated.

## Ambition

The Lab will test whether a small team can apply enterprise discipline without
enterprise bloat: clear component ownership, event contracts, zero-trust
boundaries, externalised policy, privacy by design, observable outcomes, and
repeatable assurance.

Success is not measured by the number of components built. It is measured by
whether a demonstrator makes a meaningful organisational problem easier to
understand, test, and improve—and whether an informed outsider can inspect the
evidence and reach their own conclusion.

## Position

Public Purpose Lab is an independent experimental collaboration. It does not
claim NHS, government, charity-sector, employer, or regulatory endorsement.
Initial health and care work is operational rather than clinical and uses only
synthetic data and simulated interfaces.
